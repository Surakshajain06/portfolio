About Me

I am currently working as a Junior Research Fellow (JRF) in the Department of Medical Sciences and Technology (MST) at IIT Madras under the guidance of Prof. Rupsa Bhattacharjee. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Biotechnology and an M.Tech in Clinical Engineering from IIT Madras, and I am transitioning into Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence.

I am particularly interested in the mathematical foundations of AI and Machine Learning and how these principles translate into practical algorithms and solutions. Through my research work, I am exploring their applications to healthcare-related problems.

My long-term goal is to pursue R&D in AI and Machine Learning for healthcare, bridging mathematical understanding, computational methods, and clinical applications to contribute to meaningful healthcare solutions.
